## Goal

Deliver a 4-day practical workshop on the **agentic research workflow** (BIDS + DataLad + Snakemake + projio + worklog) to the department in **September 2026**. Each participant leaves with a BIDS+DataLad copy of their own dataset, a working Snakemake pipeline, a configured projio + Claude Code setup, and one task in their workflow now agent-assisted.

## Why this goal matters

- **Forces a coherent public version** of Arash's living workflow, which has been improving privately without external pressure.
- **Anchors the handbook timeline** — without this deadline, handbook work drifts indefinitely behind TAC and manuscript pressure.
- **Starts community for projio + worklog**, which the Deep Research synthesis identifies as the survival path for single-creator research software (cf. Quantomatic warning).
- **Recruits early adopters / collaborators** from the department.

## Success criteria

- ≥ 6 participants register and complete days 1–3.
- ≥ 5 participants present on day 4 with a working artifact applied to their own data.
- Workshop materials publishable as `teaching/agentic-workshop/2026-09/` website + book + slides; reusable for a 2027 iteration with deltas only.
- ≥ 1 participant adopts projio for ongoing research work post-workshop.
